Privacy Policy

Claude Google Connector is a personal tool. Only the owner uses it, with the owner's own Google accounts.

  • Data from Google (email, files, calendar) is used only to do what the owner asks.
  • Sign-in keys stay on the owner's own computer.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for ads.
  • The owner can remove access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
